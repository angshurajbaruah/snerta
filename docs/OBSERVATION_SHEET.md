# snerta — observation sheet

One per person. Print it, or copy it into a notebook. The app records times and taps;
this sheet records the things it cannot see, which is most of what matters.

---

**A note on reading this back.** Hesitation is not discomfort, and a quick tap is not a
comfortable one — this sheet holds what was visible, next to what they said about it,
and the two are allowed to disagree. Where they do, that disagreement is the finding.
Don't resolve it in the write-up by picking whichever one supports the hypothesis.
